About the game

Voxenite is a co-op survival game set in a world built from blocks 0.125 m across, eight to a metre. A cubic metre holds 512 of them, and every one can be cut, burned, soaked or dug out.

So the world answers back. A felled oak keeps the shape of its crown, a grass fire leaves a ragged scar, and water finds every low spot on its way downhill.

Voxenite is in development and changes every day. Follow it for clips and news on YouTube, X and TikTok.

  • Blocks 0.125 m across. Eight to a metre, and every one can be cut, burned, soaked or dug out.
  • Trees fall when cut. Chop through a trunk and the tree comes down the way it leans, crown and all. The stump and the log stay where they are.
  • Fire spreads. Dry grass catches from a torch and the fire runs with the wind. Damp grass only smoulders, water puts it out where it lands, and what burned stays black.
  • Water finds its level. Springs feed streams, streams fill ponds, and spilled water runs downhill and soaks away.
  • Dark means dark. Night and caves get no light but what you bring. A pitch torch burns about twenty minutes, a birch-bark torch fifteen, a twist of dry grass four.
  • Wildlife. Roe deer browse at the wood’s edge, likeliest at dawn and dusk, and bolt when they catch your scent.
  • Survival. Knap flint into blades, lay a campfire stick by stick and cook venison over it.
  • Co-op. Host a world from the menu and your friends join you, in the same world at the same time.
