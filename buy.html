What’s in the game

  • A world you can dig, cut and burn, one 0.125 m block at a time.
  • Trees that fall under your axe and keep the shape of their crown.
  • Fire that spreads and water that flows downhill.
  • Survival: gather, knap stone, build a fire and last the night.
  • Torches you wrap yourself from birch bark or dry grass, and nights that are truly dark.
  • Roe deer at the edge of the woods, and wary of your scent.
  • Creative mode, from the pause menu.
  • Co-op with your friends.
