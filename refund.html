Refunds

If the game isn’t for you, you can have your money back within 30 days of buying it. You don’t need to give a reason.

How to ask

Reply to your Paddle receipt, or go to paddle.net and look up your order. Paddle is the Merchant of Record for your order, so Paddle makes the refund, back to the way you paid. You can also email voxenitegame@gmail.com and we’ll pass it on.

After 30 days

Tell us what went wrong and we’ll look at it. We can’t promise a refund after 30 days, unless the law where you live gives you one.

After a refund

Your license ends, so please delete the game. We may refuse further refunds to anyone who keeps buying and refunding. Your rights under the law where you live aren’t changed by this page.
