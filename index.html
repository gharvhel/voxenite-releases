The scale

0.125 m blocks. Eight to a metre.

A cubic metre of Voxenite holds 512 of them, and every one can be cut, burned, soaked or dug out. That is why a felled oak keeps the shape of its crown, why a grass fire leaves a ragged scar, and why water finds every low spot on its way downhill.

The game

TreesFireWaterLightWildlifeSurvivalTogether

01 · Trees

Cut it and it falls.

Chop through a trunk and the tree comes down the way it leans, crown and all, and lands where it lands. What you cut stays cut: the stump, the log, the gap in the canopy.

02 · Fire

Dry grass catches.

Touch a torch to cured grass and the fire spreads blade to blade, faster with the wind. Damp grass only smoulders, water puts it out where it lands, and what burned stays black.

03 · Water

Water finds its level.

Springs feed streams and streams fill ponds. Spill water and it spreads thin, runs downhill and soaks into sand and soil, quickly or slowly, the way real ground drinks it.

04 · Light

Dark means dark.

Night and caves get no light but what you bring. A pitch torch burns about twenty minutes, one rolled from birch bark about fifteen, and a twist of dry grass just four. A campfire lights everything round it for as long as you feed it.

05 · Wildlife

The wild has its own business.

Roe deer browse at the wood’s edge and bolt when they catch your scent on the wind, so come at them from downwind. They are likeliest at dawn and dusk.

06 · Survival

Live off the land.

Knap flint into a blade. Lay a fire from dry grass, sticks and dead branches and light it with your torch. Hang venison beside the flames and take it off when it browns. Leave it too long and it chars.

07 · Together

Survive side by side.

Host a world from the menu and your friends join you. Fell trees, fight fires and explore together, in the same world, at the same time.

Heads-up: Windows will warn you when you open it. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Windows for now. Linux and Mac later.

Windows will warn you first. When you run the installer, a blue “Windows protected your PC” box comes up. Windows shows it for any new program it hasn’t seen many people run, and that hasn’t got a paid signing certificate yet. It doesn’t mean Windows found anything wrong. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

“Smart App Control blocked an app”? Some Windows 11 PCs have Smart App Control on, and it gives no “Run anyway” button. We’re getting the installer signed so it simply opens there too. Until then you can turn it off in Windows Security → App & browser control → Smart App Control. Know before you do: Voxenite only runs while it stays off (turning it back on blocks the game again), and since the October 2026 Windows update some PCs can’t switch it back on from that screen. If you’d rather wait, tell us on the Discord and we’ll let you know the day the signed installer is up.

Want to be sure first? Upload the installer to VirusTotal before you run it. It’s free, and it checks the file with about seventy virus scanners.

Getting started

  1. Run Voxenite-Setup.exe. When Windows warns you, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Keep.
  2. Click Install. That’s all it asks.
  3. Make an account in the game, with your email and a password.
  4. Enter your key. You only do this once.

What you need

  • 8 GB of memory and a 4-core processor
  • A graphics card with Vulkan drivers. A GTX 1060 or RX 580 is the least we’d try, and an RTX 3060 or better plays best. We’re still finding out what it takes, so tell us how it runs.
  • Playing with friends over the internet needs a forwarded port on the host’s router for now. Your worlds stay on your PC.
